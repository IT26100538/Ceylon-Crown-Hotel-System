About Us

A Crown Jewel of Island Hospitality

Welcome to Ceylon Crown Hotel, a luxurious beachfront retreat nestled along the golden shores of Bentota, Sri Lanka. Surrounded by stunning ocean views, tropical palm trees, and beautiful sandy beaches, our hotel offers the perfect escape for every traveller. Whether you are planning a romantic honeymoon, a relaxing family holiday, an adventurous getaway with friends, or a peaceful solo trip, Ceylon Crown Hotel provides the perfect blend of comfort, elegance, and Sri Lankan hospitality. Enjoy stylish accommodation, delightful dining experiences, breathtaking sunsets, and memorable moments by the sea. With a special touch of romance for honeymoon couples and a welcoming atmosphere for travellers from around the world, we aim to make every stay truly unforgettable. Discover the beauty of Bentota and experience a tropical getaway where luxury meets the ocean.
